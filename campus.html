🎯 Advice

Freshers' Survival Tips

A collection of survival advice compiled by senior ranks to guide you through your first semester.

01

Manage Your Time Wisely

Balance classes, labs, and personal study loops. Leverage tools like Notion or calendars to log deadlines early.

02

Stay Organised

Keep your hostel room clean, stack textbooks neatly, and back up code assignments to secure digital folders.

03

Participate Actively

Onboard into technical and creative clubs early. Active contributions build leadership skills beyond books.

04

Maintain Good Attendance

Attend lectures regularly. Keeping your attendance above the mandatory 75% baseline protects exam status.

05

Take Care of Your Health

Eat healthy meals at the hostel mess, stay hydrated, and target 7-8 hours of sleep to keep cognitive focus high.

06

Make New Friends Early

Connect with campus peers and hostel roommates. A strong study and peer circle makes university feel like home.

07

Use Campus Resources

Consult library directories, utilize lab hours, and schedule consultations with faculty advisors for help.

08

Enjoy Every Moment

B.Tech is academically demanding, but the campus memories, friendships, and projects are what you will cherish.
