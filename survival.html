Best Practices

The Onboarding Do's List

Essential habits and proactive behaviors that will set you up for long-term academic and personal success.

01

Attend Classes Regularly

Ensure you satisfy attendance guidelines (>75% mandatory) to prevent registration locks and negative internal impacts.

02

Complete Assignments Early

Don't wait for last-minute rushes. Tackling class assignments early prevents backlog buildup and reduces stress.

03

Make New Friends Genuinely

Build a diverse and authentic support network. Peer groups make coding collaborations and study sessions much more effective.

04

Join Clubs and Events

Engage in hackathons, robotics, dance, or sports. Extracurriculars build communication, leadership, and portfolio visibility.

05

Explore New Skills

Use online platforms, workshops, and project-based challenges to self-learn technologies not covered in your classes.

06

Seek Help When Stuck

Reach out to seniors, lab assistants, or faculty mentors. Asking questions early saves hours of unproductive struggling.

Critical Warnings

Common Fresher Don'ts

Crucial traps, bad habits, and shortcuts that seniors recommend avoiding at all costs during your first year.

⚠ Warning
✕
😴

Skipping Classes

Missing lectures leads directly to academic deficits, failed surprise tests, and stressful attendance corrections near endsems.

⚠ Warning
✕
📅

Studying Only Before Exams

Cramming the night before an exam might get you a passing grade, but it results in poor concept retention and terrible CGPAs.

⚠ Warning
✕
📝

Ignoring Internal Marks

Internals contribute 50% or more of your total course grade. Disregarding them makes securing an 'A' grade almost impossible.

⚠ Warning
✕
🌀

Poor Time Management

Spending all your hours gaming or socializing will quickly cause you to fall behind. Develop balance from week one.

⚠ Warning
✕
🙈

Avoiding Participation

Staying confined to your hostel room limits your growth. Step out, volunteer, participate in discussions, and code together.

⚠ Warning
✕
🥱

Neglecting Your Health

Sacrificing meals and sleeping only 3 hours per night will ruin your immune system. Physical health directly dictates mental efficiency.

Learning Pathways

Academic Success Tips

A breakdown of first-year subject categories and strategies to approach them effectively.

📖

Theory Subjects

Requires building conceptual understanding, memorizing key frameworks, and clear written expressions on examinations.

  • Attend all lectures and take structured, handwritten class notes.
  • Review course slides on the same day to reinforce concepts.
  • Prepare custom summary sheets two weeks before major mid-sems.
  • Practice writing long answers and solve past question papers.
💻

Programming Courses

Requires logical thinking, code-writing speed, and systematic debugging methods. Focus heavily on practical syntax and libraries.

  • Write code daily for at least 30 minutes to build muscle memory.
  • Solve programming problems on platforms like LeetCode or HackerRank.
  • Build small personal projects to apply theory to real applications.
  • Study syntax error outputs to learn how to debug systems.
🔬

Practical Labs

Focuses on experimental setups, hardware/software implementation, and accurate analysis of results.

  • Read the lab manual thoroughly before stepping into class.
  • Draft and double-check code logic at your room before lab hours.
  • Actively run experiments yourself rather than relying on partners.
  • Maintain regular, neat records and submit lab logs on time.
