Policy

📋 Attendance Policy

Maintaining consistent attendance ensures steady understanding, project participation, and eligibility to write examinations.

📅

Attend Classes Consistently

Every single lecture, tutorial, and practical session is recorded online. Aim to attend all classes to keep concept gaps to a minimum.

📊

Monitor Attendance Regularly

Check logs on your AUMS dashboard weekly. Bring any mismatch anomalies to the attention of faculty guides immediately.

📢

Stay Informed on Updates

Read all announcements from the Dean's office and mentors concerning calendar changes, extra classes, and academic updates.

✅

Meet Minimum Requirements

Maintain a minimum threshold of 75% attendance in every course. Lower ratios trigger warnings and disqualify writing final exams.

Pro Advice

💡 Tips to Maintain a High CGPA

Actionable strategies suggested by senior rank-holders to score consistently well throughout your B.Tech journey.

🏫

Attend Classes Regularly

Being present keeps you updated with concepts that form the basis of upcoming continuous evaluations.

🔁

Revise Daily

Allocate 30 minutes every evening to review slide datasets and program scripts run in the lab sessions.

⏰

Complete Assignments on Time

Avoid last-minute rushes. Submitting worksheets early maintains strong internal evaluation records.

💻

Practice Coding Daily

Use platforms like LeetCode or GitHub to write algorithms. Hands-on coding saves time in practical tests.

📖

Prepare for Internals Early

Don't wait for schedules. Standard study preparations should start at least a week before midterm days.

🙋

Seek Faculty Help When Needed

Utilize faculty guidance hours to clear difficult doubts about code, system architectures, or theories.

👥

Join Study Groups

Collaborate with classmates. Explaining complex code logic to others is a great way to verify your own understanding.

🎯

Set Clear Academic Goals

Plan targets for exams from day one. Breaking down syllabus goals will keep motivation strong.
